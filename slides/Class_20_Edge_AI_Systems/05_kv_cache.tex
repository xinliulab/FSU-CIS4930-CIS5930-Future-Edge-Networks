\section[KV cache]{The KV cache: memory that grows with the conversation}
% =====================================================================
\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Why decoding keeps a KV cache}
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.52\textwidth}
\[
o_t=\mathrm{softmax}\!\Big(\frac{q_t\,K_{1:t}^{\top}}{\sqrt{d}}\Big)\,V_{1:t}
\]
\small
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{4pt}
  \item The keys and values of past tokens never change, so store them and append one row per step.
  \item Without the cache, every step recomputes all $t$ tokens: $O(t^2)$ total work. With it, each step does $O(t)$ work.
  \item The price: memory grows \emph{linearly} with context, and every decode step reads the whole cache. That is bandwidth again.
\end{itemize}
\column{0.45\textwidth}
\vspace{18pt}

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{How big is the KV cache?}
\vspace{-4pt}
\[
\text{KV bytes} \;=\; 2\times L\times H_{kv}\times d_{\text{head}}\times n_{\text{ctx}}\times \text{bytes/elem}
\]
\begin{center}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{@{}l@{\hspace{1.4em}}l@{\hspace{1.4em}}l@{}}
$L$: layers & $H_{kv}$: key/value heads per layer & $d_{\text{head}}$: dimension per head \\
$n_{\text{ctx}}$: tokens so far (prompt $+$ output) & $2$: one K and one V & bytes/elem: 2 for FP16, 0.5 for INT4 \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\vspace{-3pt}
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.43\textwidth}
\small
\textbf{Llama-2-7B}: $L{=}32$, $H_{kv}{=}32$, $d_{\text{head}}{=}128$\\
$\Rightarrow$ 512 KiB/token in FP16; 2 GiB at 4K tokens.

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{Llama-3-8B} (GQA): $H_{kv}{=}8$\\
$\Rightarrow$ 128 KiB/token; 1 GiB at 8K, \textbf{16 GiB at 128K}.

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Four knobs to shrink the KV cache}
\small
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.49\textwidth}
\begin{block}{1.\ Fewer KV heads \hfill $H_{kv}$}
MQA shares one K/V head across all query heads. \textbf{GQA} (EMNLP'23) shares one per group: near-MHA quality at near-MQA speed. Llama-3-8B: 32 query heads, 8 KV heads $\Rightarrow$ 4$\times$ smaller.
\end{block}
\begin{block}{3.\ Fewer bits \hfill bytes/elem}
\textbf{KIVI} (ICML'24): 2-bit KV cache, keys per channel and values per token. Almost the same quality with 2.6$\times$ less peak memory.
\end{block}
\column{0.49\textwidth}
\begin{block}{2.\ Fewer tokens \hfill $n_{\text{ctx}}$}
\textbf{H2O} (NeurIPS'23) keeps ``heavy-hitter'' tokens plus recent ones. \textbf{StreamingLLM} (ICLR'24) keeps a few ``attention-sink'' tokens plus a sliding window.
\end{block}
\begin{block}{4.\ Better placement \hfill where it lives}
\textbf{PagedAttention} (SOSP'23) pages the cache like an OS to stop fragmentation. \textbf{KVSwap} (MobiSys'26, on 11/4) keeps it on flash and prefetches what is needed next.
\end{block}
\end{columns}
\vspace{2pt}
The KV cache is your conversation in another form, so it is a privacy target too (KV-Cloak, 11/23).
\refline{Shazeer, arXiv 2019 (MQA) \textperiodcentered{} Ainslie et al., EMNLP 2023 \textperiodcentered{} Z.~Zhang et al., NeurIPS 2023 \textperiodcentered{} G.~Xiao et al., ICLR 2024 \textperiodcentered{} Z.~Liu et al., ICML 2024 \textperiodcentered{} Kwon et al., ACM SOSP 2023 \textperiodcentered{} H.~Zhang et al., ACM MobiSys 2026. Full titles in the References.}
\note{28:45--30:30. The four knobs map to the four factors of the formula: H_kv, n_ctx, bytes/elem, and where the bytes live.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Exercise: does it fit in 4 GB?}
\vspace{4pt}
A \textbf{3B} GQA model: $L{=}28$, $H_{kv}{=}8$, $d_{\text{head}}{=}128$. App memory budget: \textbf{4 GB}. Phone bandwidth: $\approx$\textbf{60 GB/s}.
\vspace{4pt}
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{6pt}
  \item Weights at INT4 (g128, $\approx$4.25 bits)?\\
    \uncover<2->{\alert{$3\times10^9\times4.25/8\approx$ \textbf{1.6 GB}}}
  \item KV cache per token in FP16? For 8K tokens? For 32K tokens?\\
    \uncover<3->{\alert{$2\cdot28\cdot8\cdot128\cdot2$ B = \textbf{112 KiB}/token, so $\approx$0.9 GB at 8K and $\approx$3.8 GB at 32K}}
  \item Does a 32K-token chat fit?\\
    \uncover<4->{\alert{$1.6+3.8>4$ GB \no. With a 4-bit KV cache ($\approx$0.95 GB) \yes, or evict or offload tokens.}}
  \item Decode upper bound at 8K context?\\
    \uncover<5->{\alert{Each token reads $\approx1.6+0.9=2.5$ GB, so at most $60/2.5\approx$ \textbf{24 tok/s}.}}
\end{enumerate}
\note{30:30--33:00. Give one minute in pairs, then reveal. Q4 shows that the KV cache costs speed as well as space.}
\end{frame}
