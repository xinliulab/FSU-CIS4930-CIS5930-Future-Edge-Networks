\vspace{6pt}
\begin{block}{Routing, twice}
\small
\textbf{Network routing} picks a path per flow from link costs.\\
\textbf{Model routing} picks a model per request from \emph{predicted} quality and cost.\\[2pt]
The hard part: predicting quality \emph{before} paying for the big model.
\end{block}
\end{columns}
\note{40:00--41:00. Link to the course theme: model routing and network routing are the same optimization with different costs.}

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{How routers predict quality}
\small
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.32\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Cascade: try small first}
\textbf{AutoMix} (NeurIPS'24): the small model answers and checks itself (few-shot self-verification). A POMDP router escalates when it is unsure.\\[2pt]
$\Rightarrow$ over 50\% lower cost at comparable performance.\\[2pt]
\con hard queries pay twice.
\end{block}
\column{0.32\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Predict difficulty upfront}
\textbf{Hybrid LLM} (ICLR'24): a router predicts the quality gap between the small and large model for each query. A threshold, tunable at test time, trades quality for cost.\\[2pt]
$\Rightarrow$ up to 40\% fewer calls to the large model with no quality drop.
\end{block}
\column{0.32\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Learn from preferences}
\textbf{RouteLLM} (ICLR'25): routers trained on human preference data, with data augmentation.\\[2pt]
$\Rightarrow$ over 2$\times$ lower cost without losing quality, and the routers transfer to unseen model pairs.
\end{block}
\end{columns}
\vspace{6pt}
All three were built around cloud cost. At the edge, ``cost'' also includes battery, RTT, and privacy.
\refline{P.~Aggarwal et al., AutoMix: Automatically Mixing Language Models, NeurIPS 2024 \textperiodcentered{} D.~Ding et al., Hybrid LLM: Cost-Efficient and Quality-Aware Query Routing, ICLR 2024 \textperiodcentered{}
I.~Ong et al., RouteLLM: Learning to Route LLMs from Preference Data, ICLR 2025}
\note{41:00--42:15. Three ways to predict quality, ordered by when the prediction happens: after, before, and learned offline.}
\end{frame}
