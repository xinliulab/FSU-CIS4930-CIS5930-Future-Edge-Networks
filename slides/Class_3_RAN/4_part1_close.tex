\begin{frame}{Monday, in five sentences}
  \vspace{0.5em}
  \small
  \begin{enumerate}\itemsep7pt
    \item The icon on your phone names a radio, and somebody pays about \$50 a
      month to put it there.
    \item Every generation made one new habit feel normal: voice, text, web,
      video.
    \item The network has always had the same jobs --- find you, admit you,
      move your bits, bill you.
    \item A box splits in two when its halves need to change at different speeds.
    \item Your phone attaches to the loudest tower, and in 2G nobody checked
      whether that tower was honest.
  \end{enumerate}

  \vspace{0.8em}
  \takeaway{Sentence four is the one that will keep paying off.}
\end{frame}
